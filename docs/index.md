## What is PriEco?

PriEco is a general purpose web search engine. Its goal is find the best results for your queries and questions. That's it. We keep you private while searching, we run on renewable energy, we are transparent about how PriEco works and we develop what our users want.

## Mission & Vision

To build a truly fair and well made web search engine for everyone. At the start founder, Roman Láncoš, was frustrated with Google alternatives and didn't understand why already established alternatives didn't improve in ways other alternatives already did. Always creating sacrafices and specialized web search engines. For example Ecosia, it's cool it plants trees but why does it have to share user data with Microsoft (missing privacy concept) and doesn't use the best index: Google.

PriEco is built to fullfill all of these concepts.

- **Privacy**, inspired by DuckDuckGo

PriEco never stores nor shares your activity and any potential telemery is opt-in

- **Ecology**, inspired by Ecosia

[PriEco](https://prieco.net/) fully runs on renewable energy

- **Open source**

PriEco user convinced Roman Láncoš to make PriEco open source under AGPL license during its 1st year of existance

You can find source code [here](https://codeberg.org/JojoYou/PriEco/)

- **Independent**

After many years of keeping PriEco a [meta search engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metasearch_engine), Roman Láncoš fully realized the approach was unscalable and own index needed to be created

- **Decentralization**

An upcomming concept

Main reason is to grow index to billions of pages which could make PriEco competitive with already big, established indexes and web search engines

Amazing additional benefits are:

- Self-hostability of nodes
- Resiliance against cencorship
